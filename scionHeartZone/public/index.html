Cyan Heart is a horror exploration puzzle adventure with one strange rule: the camera always points back at her. The path ahead is the one thing you cannot see.

    Check behind her and under her feet through the screen. Whatever is following, you will see it before she does.

[ LOG 01 ]

A world dyed cyan

Magenta Heart

Life once ran on a peculiar energy that could mimic any physical phenomenon, forever.

  • Mimic the heat of a flame and it becomes a heat source that never burns out.
  • Mimic a heavy blow and it becomes a rock drill that never stops.

Cyan

Then, one day, the energy changed color. It still works. It is no longer stable.

  • Anything with a physical form is turned into cyan matter.
  • Machines and living things alike are warped by it.

[ LOG 02 ]

Into the depths of an abandoned facility

Rumor says something inside can stop the spread of Cyan. She goes in alone.

Because Cyan is eternal, the empty facility keeps behaving as if its occupants never left. Doors still cycle. Machines still work their shifts. Nobody is there.

Avoid what Cyan is mimicking, or turn it to your advantage, and bring back the object said to wait at the very end.
